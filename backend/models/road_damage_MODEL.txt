RoadRead road-damage model
Model: YOLOv8s, fine-tuned on RDD2022 road-damage data
Classes: D00 Longitudinal Crack; D10 Transverse Crack; D20 Alligator Crack; D40 Pothole
Checkpoint path: backend/models/road_damage/yolov8s_rdd2022.pt
Source: https://huggingface.co/SreekarAditya/yolo-rdd2022-benchmark

For offline deployment, run:
    python download_model.py
or set ROAD_DAMAGE_MODEL_PATH in backend/.env to a local .pt checkpoint.
